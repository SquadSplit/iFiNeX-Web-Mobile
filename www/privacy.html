iFiNeX — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Website: https://squadsplit.github.io

1. Who we are

iFiNeX (formerly Squad Split) is a personal and group finance tracker (bill tracker, squad expense splitting, expense trackers, budget planner). It is run by its owner as a private service for invited users. Contact: abuabdullah.be@gmail.com.

2. Data we store

  • Account data: your e-mail address, display name, emoji/colour and role, used to sign you in with an e-mail one-time code.
  • Your content: cards, bills, payments, expenses, squads and their members, trackers, budgets and notifications that you or your squad enter.
  • Device data for alerts: an anonymous device identifier and push-notification keys, only if you turn alerts on.

This data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase and is visible only to you, to the people you share a card or squad with, and to the service administrator.

3. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose Settings → Backup & Restore → Google Drive backup, you sign in on Google's own page and allow iFiNeX to use your Google account. iFiNeX requests only these permissions:

  • openid and email — to show you which Google account is connected.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — lets iFiNeX create, read, list and delete only the files it created itself, inside a folder named “iFiNeX Backups” in your Google Drive. iFiNeX cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

What we do with it: on the schedule you choose (daily, weekly, monthly or custom) our server creates a backup file of your own iFiNeX data and uploads it to that folder, and removes the oldest backup files when you exceed the number you chose to keep. You can also back up or restore manually.

How the access is protected: Google gives us a refresh token. It is stored encrypted on our server, is never sent to your phone or browser, and is used only for the actions above. We do not sell, rent, share or use Google user data for advertising, and no person reads your Drive data or your backup files.

Limited Use disclosure: iFiNeX's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Stopping and deleting

  • Tap Disconnect Google Drive in the app. The stored token is deleted and access is revoked at Google. You can also remove iFiNeX at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Backup files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them yourself.
  • To delete your iFiNeX account and data, e-mail the contact address above from your registered address. We will delete it within 30 days, except records other squad members need for shared totals, which are anonymised where possible.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with (a) people you add to a card or squad, and (b) infrastructure providers needed to run the service (Supabase for database and functions, GitHub Pages for the website, Google for the optional Drive backup you enable).

6. Security and retention

Data is protected by access rules in the database so each person can read only what they are allowed to. Google tokens are encrypted at rest. We keep data while your account is active.

7. Children

iFiNeX is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. Contact: abuabdullah.be@gmail.com. See also the Terms of Service.
